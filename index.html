About

I study how brains and models support flexible, continual learning.

I am a PhD student at Caltech, where I began in September 2025. My interests span neural computation, cognitive flexibility, generalization from limited examples, and imagination. Earlier work at CMU and MIT shaped how I think about computation across scales.
